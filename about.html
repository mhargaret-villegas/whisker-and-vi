Meet the Owner

Mhargaret Villegas

Hi! I'm the owner of Whisker and Vi. I created this cat-inspired shop for cat lovers and their furry companions. Our products include nutritious cat food, tasty treats, and grooming and hygiene essentials designed for everyday cat care.

♡ This is my baby! ♡

My Story

Whisker and Vi was inspired by my love for cats since childhood and the special connection people share with their furry companions. I wanted to create a shop where cat lovers could find products that support their pets' everyday needs at the comfort of their homes.

From Vi's PurrBites and Whislime to PurrTreats and WhisCare, every product represents an important part of a cat's happy and healthy life. Whisker and Vi is more than just a shop— it's a little space created with love for every whiskered friend. ᓚᘏᗢ
